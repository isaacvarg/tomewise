Lesson

K and M

K is dah-di-dah and M is dah-dah. Listen for the rhythm, not the dots and dashes. Read more in the reference sheet.

Practice

  • Say each letter as you hear it.
  • Press Space to replay.

Timing

  1. A dah is three dits long.
  2. Letters are three dits apart.
Copy behind: let the letter finish before you name it.
