Subject home order

  • Goal progress, today or this week
  • "Ask your agent" prompts, derived from state
  • Due here, with a Start for this subject
  • Roadmap: units, milestones and their lessons, then "Other lessons"
  • Drills, dimmed until one of their items is unlocked
  • Reference: the built-in glossary of items, then the agent's reference sheets
  • Charts from the subject's chart specs

What a theme targets

  • .tome-goal, .tome-goal-bar
  • .tome-prompt, .tome-say
  • .tome-milestone, .tome-status[data-status]
  • .tome-lessons [data-done], [data-later]
  • .tome-drills [data-locked], .tome-reference
  • .tome-chart with .line, .bar, .goal

Sample data

Unit 1, its milestone, lessons 1 to 4, the listening drill and the response-time field come from the Morse example. Unit 2's lessons, the spacing lesson, the second drill, both reference sheets, the goal, the explanation and the chart numbers are invented to fill the screen.
