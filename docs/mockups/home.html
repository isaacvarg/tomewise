Tomewise home, in two themes

Both phones use the same markup. Paper comes from your Morse workspace's stylesheet; Meadow is a softer nature look with leaf-shaped cells and rolling hills. Each follows your system's light or dark setting. Tap Start in Meadow to see a small piece of theme JavaScript. Below the phones, the desktop window uses the same markup again, laid out for the extra width; on a narrow screen it falls back to the phone layout.
