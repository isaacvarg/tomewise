Tomewise drill runner

A Morse listening drill on K, M, R, S and the new letter U, which comes up about half the time. Tap the circle to hear a letter at 20 WPM, then pick it. The dit-dah pattern appears only after you answer, as your notes ask. Keys work too: Space plays, and K, M, R, S, U answer in the drill you last touched. After 12 sounds you get the summary your agent would read. Below the phones, a desktop window runs the same drill as one centred column.

Runner layout

  • Top bar with close, subject, drill name and count
  • One prompt in the middle, nothing else
  • Answers within thumb reach at the bottom
  • Summary with the numbers your notes ask for

What a theme targets

  • .tome-play, [data-playing]
  • .tome-answer[data-state]
  • .tome-feedback, .tome-verdict
  • .tome-summary, .tome-stats
  • --t-good and --t-bad for results
