Tomewise glossary

Every item you have unlocked in a subject, grouped by the lesson that unlocked it, with a button to hear audio items. The app builds this page from the subject's items, so every subject gets one without the agent writing it. Your agent's own reference sheets, such as how WPM timing works, sit next to it in the subject's Reference list. Tap a play button to hear the letter at 20 WPM.
